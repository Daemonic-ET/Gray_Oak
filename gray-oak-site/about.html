Who We Are

Gray Oak Advisory is a management and technology advisory firm focused on asset management and the organizations that depend on it.

We work primarily with utilities, infrastructure owners, public agencies, asset-intensive organizations, and the companies that support them.

Our work starts from a simple observation: most organizations already have many of the pieces, and getting the pieces to work together is harder.

A good asset management strategy means little if it doesn't influence decisions. A new technology platform will not fix a process nobody understands. More data will not help if nobody knows which information matters or who owns it. And certification does not mean the work is finished. Gray Oak helps organizations work through those realities.

Founder & Principal

Melody Quiroz Laven, MBA, AMP, MIAM

“Asset management is not sexy,
but it's necessary.”

Some of the work is strategic. Some of it is decidedly not. Both matter.

Melody founded Gray Oak after more than a decade of working on the practical side of asset management, helping turn strategy, standards, data, technology, and organizational expectations into something people can use.

Most recently, she led the asset management program at a large public water and wastewater utility through ISO 55001 certification, recertification, and the work of continuing to mature the system afterward.

The work went beyond audit preparation. It crossed operations and maintenance, engineering, technology, performance measurement, internal audit, data, governance, organizational change, and the everyday problem of getting different parts of a large organization to move in the same direction.

It also taught her that asset management looks much cleaner in a framework than it does inside an organization.

Real organizations have competing priorities, systems that don't always talk to each other, imperfect data, and processes that grew around old decisions. Experienced people carry knowledge that was never written down. Sometimes everyone agrees something needs to change while nobody is sure who owns the change.

That is the work Gray Oak was created to do.

Melody is an Asset Management Professional (AMP) and Member of the Institute of Asset Management (MIAM). She holds an MBA from Ateneo de Manila Graduate School of Business and a Bachelor of Science in Community Development, with a minor in Information Technology, from the University of the Philippines.

She co-leads the South Florida Branch of the Institute of Asset Management and contributes to broader professional initiatives advancing asset management practice. She has presented and written on asset management, ISO 55001, organizational change, leadership, data, technology, and the realities of building and sustaining an asset management system.

Her approach to Gray Oak is deliberately practical: Do the work that matters. Do it well. And leave the organization more capable than you found it.
