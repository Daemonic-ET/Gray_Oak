Who We Help

Gray Oak works with people responsible for making asset-intensive organizations work.

That includes executives and asset management leaders, but also the engineering, operations, maintenance, technology, finance, procurement, customer service, and other teams whose decisions affect assets throughout their lifecycle.

Our experience is rooted in utilities and infrastructure, and our approach is relevant to other organizations where physical assets, service delivery, risk, and long-term investment matter.

Where You Are Matters

Building

Starting formal asset management, or bringing separate practices together.

Maturing

You have a program. Some parts work well and others don't.

Modernizing

Replacing a major system, improving your data, or exploring AI.

Sustaining

A mature program that now has to stay mature.

In Transition

A leadership change, reorganization, or new system shifting who owns what.

Utilities & Infrastructure Owners

Water and wastewater, electric power, energy and generation, municipal and government infrastructure, and other asset-intensive environments. The assets may be different, but many of the management questions are familiar.

What do we own, what condition is it in, and what matters most? What should we maintain, what should we replace, and where should we spend limited resources? What information do we trust, and who makes the decision? Asset management helps an organization answer those questions consistently.

Technology & Service Providers

We also work with companies that build technology and services for asset-intensive organizations.

A technically strong product can still struggle if it does not fit the way its customers make decisions or perform work. Gray Oak brings the asset owner's perspective into that conversation.

We help providers better understand asset management practices, organizational processes, information requirements, standards, and the operational context in which their products will actually be used.
