What We Do

Gray Oak provides advisory, consulting, facilitation, training, and specialized technical expertise across asset management strategy, people and change, maintenance and reliability, and data and technology. We work across the full range an organization needs — from board-level strategy to the technical detail of how a maintenance plan or a data model actually gets built.

We're practitioner-led and implementation-focused: our work is about determining how to make asset management work in practice, not just producing a report on what you should do.
